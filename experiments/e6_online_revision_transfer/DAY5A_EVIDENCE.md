## Explicit limits and remaining falsification work

- Historical and new-epoch evidence are partitioned by an explicit quarantine,
  but the onset of drift is not inferred from noisy observations.
- The output effect **signature stays fixed**. Only 0–2 conjunction guard
  literals can change; truly new action effects cannot yet be induced.
- New observations come from distinct episodes of an author-defined generator,
  not from a locked independent model family. They are not statistical proof of
  independent real-world observations.
- The learner does not autonomously choose new discriminating interventions
  during the drift epoch; they are supplied as traces.
- Observations are full Boolean snapshots; no partial visibility, stochastic
  dynamics, nonstationary switching, open-ended goals or real visual ARC tasks.
- Report success and correct rejection separately on independent future tests.
